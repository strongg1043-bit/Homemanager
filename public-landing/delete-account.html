Delete your Home Anchor account

Last updated: 30 October 2026

You can delete your Home Anchor account, and the data that goes with it, yourself. It takes under a minute and you don't need to contact us.

Delete it in the app

Parents

  1. Sign in and open the Manage tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Delete account card and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Children

  1. Sign in and open the Profile tab (bottom right).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

If your household's trial or subscription has ended and the app is locked, the same Delete my account option is at the bottom of the locked screen.

What gets deleted

  • A child's account: their profile, name, email, photo, missions and submitted proof photos, wallet and redemption history, notifications and disputes they raised, and any feature suggestions they sent.
  • A parent's account when another parent is still in the household: their profile, name, email, photo, notifications, disputes they raised and feature suggestions. Records the rest of the family relies on, such as missions they created for children and payments they recorded, stay in the household.
  • The last parent in a household: everything in the household, including every child's account and all of the data above for each of them, the household's invite code, and its settings. Any active subscription is cancelled first, so you are not charged again. The app asks you to confirm a second time before doing this.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. We can't restore an account once it has been deleted.

What we may keep

Our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy, is the merchant of record for subscriptions and may keep its own billing records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Home Anchor itself does not keep a copy of your account or household data after deletion.

Can't open the app?

If you can't sign in, for example because you've forgotten your password or lost your device, email us from the address on the account at homemanagercomms@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Home Anchor account". Tell us the name of the account holder and, for a child's account, the household name. We may ask you to confirm you own the account before we delete it, and we'll action the request within 30 days.
