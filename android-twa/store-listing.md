## Full description (max 4000 chars)

**Every home needs an anchor.**

Home Anchor turns everyday chores into a game your kids actually want to play — and gives you, the parent, a clear, fair way to track it all.

Assign tasks as daily, weekly, monthly, or one-off missions. Kids complete them, submit photo proof when it matters, and earn points the moment you approve. Those points convert to real rewards — screen time, treats, or actual cash you pay out — so effort has a payoff kids can see.

**For parents:**
- Build a task library once, then quick-assign it to any child in seconds
- Require photo proof on tasks that need it, skip it on the ones that don't
- Approve, request a retry, or resolve disputes — every decision is logged in a full audit trail
- Set your own currency, conversion rate, and minimum cash-out
- Nudge a child about an open task with one tap, and get reminders when tasks are overdue or sitting untouched

**For kids:**
- See today's missions at a glance, with points and deadlines up front
- Accept a mission, submit proof, and watch your trust score grow
- Track your wallet balance and redeem points for rewards or real cash
- A full statement of everything you've earned and spent

**Built on trust, not nagging.**
Every approval, dispute, and payment is recorded, so there's never an argument about what was agreed. Trust scores reward consistency. Nothing here is about punishment — it's about making responsibility visible and worth it.

No ads. No tracking. Your family's data stays your family's data — see our privacy policy for the full picture.

Home Anchor is built by an independent developer who wanted a better way to run chores at home. If it works for your family, we'd love to hear from you.
